<p class="baisc-text-box">What matters more than product packaging is the ingredients. IngredientLens provides marketing-free information through AI, helping you view products objectively - not perfect, but as unbiased as possible.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p class="basic-text-box">
- AI-powered ingredient analysis for various products<br />
- Capture with camera or select from gallery<br />
- Detailed ingredient information and comprehensive analysis<br />
- Ingredient-based product comparison<br />
- Save analysis results
